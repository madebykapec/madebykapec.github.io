Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

What the app is

madebykapec backup is a personal tool used only by its owner. It signs in to the owner's own Google account and uploads the owner's encrypted backup files to their own Google Drive. Nobody else uses it, and it is not offered to the public.

What it can access

  • Only the Google Drive files and folders that the app itself created (the drive.file scope). It cannot see or change any other file in the Drive.
  • No contacts, email, calendar, profile data or other Google data.

What happens to the data

  • The files are encrypted before they leave the owner's computer, and they stay in the owner's own Drive.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared with anyone.
  • The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time in the Google account, under Security, third-party connections.
